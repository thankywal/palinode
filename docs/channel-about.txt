In classical poetry, a palinode is a poem written to take back what an earlier poem said.

Palinode is an autonomous agent fleet that does the same thing for AI agents. Most agent platforms solve prevention: a policy engine, a kill switch, a trace viewer. A kill switch stops the bleeding. It does not put the blood back.

This is the other half. Palinode watches what a fleet of production agents actually did, decides on its own that something has to be taken back, and reverses it across Stripe, GitHub, Slack and a database, in reverse order of cause. Nobody presses a button.

Some actions do not come back. A settled wire transfer is one. When that happens it writes the disclosure and names the damage instead of pretending.

Built on Google ADK, Gemini 3.5 Flash on Vertex AI, Cloud Run and Firestore.
